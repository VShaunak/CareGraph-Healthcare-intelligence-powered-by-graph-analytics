Urgent Care Queue & Real-Time Capacity Boundaries

There is currently no unified, public-facing live API that reveals real-time urgent-care waiting rooms, queue counts, or triage delays in the United States. CareGraph strictly avoids estimating or guessing wait times when no certified live telemetry feed exists.

Graph Proximity & Mathematical Access Optimization

Our graph intelligence engine builds minimum-spanning proximity vectors connecting healthcare facilities. For our regional recommender, an objective composite score ranks verified facilities based on two empirical inputs:

Composite Access Score = 0.65 × Live Highway Travel Minutes + 0.35 × CMS Historical OP-18b Minutes

Because both inputs represent time delays, lower numerical scores represent superior access profiles.
